The Spirit of Cricket

Under the lights, the stadium glows,
The crowd goes wild, the tension grows.
A bat in hand, a dream in sight,
Eleven warriors ready to fight.

The bowler runs with fire and pace,
The batsman waits, calm in his place.
The ball comes flying, sharp and fast,
One perfect shot—and fear is past.

**Four runs roar across the ground,**
The cheering rises, a thunderous sound.
A six goes sailing into the sky,
And thousands of voices begin to fly.

But cricket is more than runs and fame,
More than a trophy, more than a game.
It's patience when the wickets fall,
It's standing strong after losing it all.

A spinner turns the ball with art,
A fast bowler gives every heart.
A fielder dives across the floor,
Saving one run, then fighting for more.

Sometimes the scoreboard brings a smile,
Sometimes defeat waits after every mile.
But champions learn to rise again,
Through every loss, through every pain.

The final over, the moment is near,
Every heartbeat filled with fear.
One ball remains, the target is tight,
The whole stadium holds its breath tonight.

The bat swings hard, the ball takes flight,
Straight toward the boundary—what a sight!
The crowd erupts, the players cheer,
A moment they'll remember year after year.

For cricket teaches us to believe,
To keep moving forward, never leave.
To respect the game, the team, the fight,
And chase our dreams with all our might.

So let the bat speak, let the ball fly,
Let every player reach for the sky.
Win or lose, let passion remain—
For cricket is played through joy and pain.

And when the final applause is done,
When the last ball disappears from the sun,
One truth will always remain in sight:

**Cricket is not just a game—
It's a feeling, a dream,
A battle, and a beautiful story
Written one ball at a time.** 🏏✨
